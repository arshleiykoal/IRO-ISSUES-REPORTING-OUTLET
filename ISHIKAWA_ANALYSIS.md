# IRO Ishikawa Problem Analysis

## Effect
**Ineffective reporting and resolution of issues affecting AAUA students and surrounding indigenous communities.**

## People
- Fear of victimization
- Lack of awareness
- Lack of trust
- Poor communication

## Methods
- No standardized reporting channel
- Manual reporting
- Slow follow-up
- Unclear reporting procedure

## Technology
- No centralized platform
- Lack of issue tracking
- Limited digital reporting
- No simple status mechanism

## Management
- Delayed response
- Poor coordination
- Lack of accountability
- Reports may not reach the right authority

## Environment
- Security concerns
- Poor infrastructure
- Water/electricity challenges
- Sanitation and transportation problems

## Proposed Root-Level Response
IRO provides a centralized digital channel where an issue can be submitted, categorized, assigned an ID and monitored through status updates.
